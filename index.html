Who I am

I'm Mohammad Amin, an Embedded Systems Engineer with an M.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering and more than four years of experience delivering dependable software where real-world behaviour matters. My work spans modern C++ design, real-time systems, AUTOSAR, embedded Linux, hardware bring-up, and the verification work required to make them trustworthy.
